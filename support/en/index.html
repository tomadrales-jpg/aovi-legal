AOVI Support

Updated 28 September 2026 · AOVI 2.0

AOVI is a wellbeing app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It brings together how you feel, how you sleep, how you move and what you eat, and suggests simple steps for today. AOVI guides, it doesn't diagnose.

Need help? Email us at tomadrales@hotmail.com. If you can, attach the app's support bundle (see how below).
If you're going through a very hard time, don't wait for our reply: call your local emergency number (112 in Europe, 911 in the US and Canada) or a crisis line such as 988 in the US. Inside AOVI, "Immediate help" in the Coach shows the lines for your country.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can use AOVI without an account: your data stays on your iPhone. You only need an account for the AOVI server Coach, to keep a copy of your metrics on our server, and for AOVI PRO. You can create one whenever you like in Profile → Account and AI.

What does AOVI PRO include?

AOVI PRO is a monthly or yearly subscription. It includes Trends and insights, Your week and Your month in full with their patterns, patterns in the report for your doctor, and a copy of your metrics on the AOVI server. Everything else (check-in, measures, day plan, Coach, breathing, water, journal, Presence, Apple Watch, widgets and Siri) is free.

To see it, open the Progress tab and tap "Discover PRO" or "Unlock with PRO".

I paid for PRO and I don't see it. How do I restore my purchase?

  1. Check that your iPhone uses the same Apple Account you bought it with.
  2. Open the PRO screen (Progress tab → "Discover PRO").
  3. Tap "Restore purchases".

If it says no purchases were found, email us with the date of the purchase. We don't need your payment details: purchases are handled by Apple.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → AOVI. You can also do it from AOVI, on the PRO screen, with "Manage subscription". If you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, it won't renew, and you keep PRO until that date.

Deleting your AOVI account does not cancel the subscription: cancel it separately, as above.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Go to Profile → Account and AI → Your data → "Delete account and data".

  • If you have an account, AOVI first asks our server to delete it. Only when the server confirms does it also delete the data on this iPhone. If the server doesn't respond, the app tells you and deletes nothing, so you can try again.
  • If you use AOVI without an account, the same button deletes the data on this iPhone.
  • What the Coach has learned about you is deleted separately, in the same place, with "Clear Coach memory".

The weather doesn't show up

AOVI checks the weather with Apple Weather using your approximate location, only while you use the app. If you don't see it:

  1. On your iPhone: Settings → AOVI → Location, and choose "While Using the App".
  2. Or inside AOVI: Profile → Health and permissions → Location → "Ask".
  3. Check that you're online.

In Profile → Advanced → Status, the "Weather" row tells you what's wrong: whether it works, the permission is missing, or there's no connection.

I'd rather not see numbers

Turn on Profile → Preferences → Experience → "Hide scores". You'll see words instead of numbers for your measures (Balance, Body, Mind and Nutrition), including in widgets, on Apple Watch and in Siri. Health data, such as hours of sleep, stays visible. The report for your doctor does include the numbers, and the app warns you before creating it.

How does the Coach work?

  • Coach on your iPhone. On iPhones with iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence, the Coach answers on the iPhone itself, with no account and without sending anything to our server.
  • AOVI server Coach. With an account, the Coach can also answer from our server. It only sends what you allow, separately for text, voice, photos and health signals. You can change those permissions in Profile → Account and AI → Privacy and AI.
  • The Coach suggests and you decide: it never acts on its own.
  • AOVI guides, it doesn't diagnose. For any health concern, talk to a professional.

Why does it ask for Health, Calendar or Screen Time?

All of it is optional, and AOVI works without it. Health is used to work out your measures; the calendar, to suggest free slots; and Screen Time, to know whether you went over 1, 2 or 3 hours today in the apps you choose (AOVI can't see which apps they are or the minutes). The details are in the privacy policy.

How to send us the support bundle

The support bundle helps us find the problem. It contains technical information (version, settings, permissions and recent errors). It masks emails, tokens and passwords, and doesn't include the content of network requests. It only leaves your iPhone if you send it.

  1. Open Profile → Support and diagnostics.
  2. Under "Help", tap "Generate support bundle".
  3. Choose Mail and send it to tomadrales@hotmail.com. Tell us what happened and when.

If the app showed you a support code (8 letters and numbers), include it in your email. You'll see it next to the error and in the list of recent errors in Profile → Support and diagnostics.

You can also email us from Profile → Support and diagnostics → "Contact support": the email opens with the app version already filled in.
