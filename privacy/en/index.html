AOVI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026 · Version 2.2 · For AOVI 2.0

AOVI is a wellbeing app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what data the app handles, what leaves your iPhone, where it goes, with which permission, and how you can withdraw it. It describes what the app does, not what it might do. If this translation and the Spanish version ever differ, the Spanish version applies.

The short version.
  • Your data lives on your iPhone, and you can use AOVI without an account.
  • The Coach that answers on your iPhone (with Apple Intelligence) sends nothing.
  • With AOVI PRO, if the AOVI Coach isn't available, Apple's AI can answer: on your iPhone or, if you allow it, on Apple's Private Cloud Compute (section 3).
  • Nothing about your health goes to the AI on our server unless you turn on that specific permission.
  • Your journal is encrypted and never leaves your iPhone. Neither do your clothing photos or your skin and hair selfies.
  • The app sends anonymous crash diagnostics, which you can turn off. Usage counts are only sent if you agree.
  • We don't sell data. No ads, no cross-app tracking.

1 · Data controller and who else is involved

Controller: Tomás López Álvarez. Privacy contact: tomadrales@hotmail.com. You can write to that address to exercise any of the rights in section 9.

Our server

When something leaves your iPhone for AOVI, it goes to our own server, run by AOVI's developer. It is located in Spain. It is reachable over the internet through an encrypted connection (HTTPS), via the Tailscale Funnel service. AOVI does not use iCloud to store your data.

Who else handles data

  • vidAI processes requests to the server Coach (section 3). vidAI is the AOVI developer's own AI infrastructure, on the same server in Spain: your data is not shared with third parties for the Coach.
  • Apple, as a processor, for weather (Apple Weather), purchases (App Store), Sign in with Apple and Health. Apple handles that data under its own policy.
  • Apple, also, for Private Cloud Compute, if you have AOVI PRO and the AOVI Coach isn't available (section 3). We don't receive or keep those conversations.

Account (optional)

Without an account, everything stays on your iPhone. You only need an account for the server Coach, for the copy of your metrics on our server and for AOVI PRO. With an account, our server keeps your email and what it needs to check your password (or, with Apple, its identifier and your name if you share it), a random identifier for your iPhone, the app version, and an access log (IP address and device type).

Photos and camera

  • Meals. With the "Photos" permission, the photo you choose is scaled down and sent to the server Coach to estimate calories and nutrients. It is not stored. Saving the result to Health always needs your confirmation.
  • Clothing (Presence). The cut-out, colour and garment type are worked out on the iPhone. If a face appears in the photo, it is covered before saving.
  • Skin and hair selfie. Face points (eyes, tilt) are worked out on the iPhone to analyse the skin. They are not stored separately from the photo and never leave the iPhone.
  • Presence photos are stored encrypted, with a key that only exists on this iPhone, and are excluded from iCloud backup.
  • Sample wardrobe. If you ask for it, the app asks the server for a sample wardrobe and downloads its photos. To do that it sends your style (womenswear, menswear or neutral) and, with the "Health signals" permission, your colour season, worked out on the iPhone from the selfie. The style comes from what you choose in the app, not from Health data.
  • Save image. Cards you share are only saved to Photos if you ask.

Screen Time

It is optional. You pick the apps with Apple's picker. AOVI can't see which apps they are or how many minutes you use them: it only knows whether you went over 1, 2 or 3 hours in them today. It uses this on the iPhone to work out your Mind measure.

Calendar, Reminders and HomeKit

  • Calendar: the app reads your events on the iPhone to show them, warn you about clashes and suggest free slots, and creates events when you plan. The Coach only gets the summary from the table above.
  • Reminders: only if you turn it on, to export your shopping list and tasks.
  • HomeKit: only if you turn it on, to dim the lights of your meditation scene and put them back when you finish. The names of your home and room stay on the iPhone.

Location and Apple Weather

With your permission, and only while you use the app, AOVI asks for your approximate location to check the weather with Apple Weather (Apple is the processor). It uses the weather to adjust your outfit, your plan and your water for the day. Your location goes to Apple Weather, not to our server, and no history is kept.

3 · What leaves your device, and with which permission

The Coach with Apple's AI (AOVI PRO)

The Coach answers from our server first. With AOVI PRO, if it can't be reached (no network, no account or the server is down), Apple's AI can answer instead. You can turn this off in Profile → Account and AI → Apple AI, and each answer shows who gave it.

  • On your iPhone. On iPhones with iOS 26 and Apple Intelligence, Apple's model answers on the iPhone itself. Those conversations don't leave your iPhone.
  • Private Cloud Compute. Only if you turn on "Allow Private Cloud Compute" (it is off by default), on iOS 27, with a network connection and the "Text and context" permission, the same message and context that would go to our server can go to Private Cloud Compute, Apple's servers for Apple Intelligence. Apple processes them to answer and, according to Apple, doesn't keep them; Apple handles that data under its own policy. We don't receive or keep that conversation. If you turn it off again, only the iPhone answers. Apple sets a daily usage limit.

The sentence of the day on Today and, with AOVI PRO, the "Your week" summary are also written by the iPhone's model, from your measures and without anything leaving it.

The server Coach (with an account)

With an account, the Coach can also answer from our server, processed by vidAI. Allowing the microphone or Health in iOS does not mean allowing data to be sent: each purpose is granted separately in Profile → Account and AI → Privacy and AI, is recorded with its date and version, and can be withdrawn at any time with immediate effect.

Day plan and memory (AOVI PRO)

With AOVI PRO and the "Health signals" permission, the app can ask the server to improve your day plan and to summarise what it knows about you. It sends your measures, the time, today's tasks, the habits it has learned and its own proposal. Never your journal or your Health history. What AOVI knows about you can be viewed, edited and deleted on the iPhone, in Profile → Account and AI → What AOVI knows about you.

Recommendation feedback (with an account)

When you rate a recommendation, the app sends our server which one it was, its module and title, what you did and when. Your WHO-5 score is only included if you have given the "Health signals" permission.

Copy of your metrics on the server (AOVI PRO, optional)

If you turn it on in Profile → Advanced → Cloud Sync, once a day the app uploads a summary to our server: your wellbeing score, how many WHO-5 check-ins you've done and the latest score, counts of your recommendations, the app version, language, time zone, system, iPhone model and a random identifier for the iPhone. It is a copy: today the app doesn't use it to restore your data on another iPhone.

4 · Health data (HealthKit)

AOVI only reads what you allow from Health: sleep, heart rate and its variability, resting heart rate, high, low or irregular heart rate notifications, respiration, blood oxygen, wrist temperature, steps, activity, energy, effort and workouts, time in daylight, weight, height, VO₂ max, state of mind, mindful minutes, nutrition and, if they are in Health, the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 questionnaires. With your permission it also reads your date of birth and sex to calibrate the measures and the Monitor figure. It uses all this on the iPhone to work out Body, Mind and Nutrition.

AOVI writes water, reviewed meals (energy and nutrients, caffeine and alcohol), mindful minutes and your state of mind to Health when you log or confirm them. The Apple Watch reads your heart rate during guided breathing and saves the mood and water you log.

Menstrual cycle (optional). Only if you turn it on, AOVI reads the period days you have logged in Health and estimates your cycle phase on the iPhone (menstrual, follicular, ovulatory or luteal) to adapt its training, food and rest suggestions. The app offers it to people whose sex in Health is female, and anyone whose sex in Health is not male can turn it on or off in Profile → Health and permissions → Menstrual cycle. It is a calendar estimate for wellbeing purposes: it is not a method of contraception or fertility tracking, and it is not a diagnosis. AOVI doesn't read symptoms or any other reproductive health data, and writes nothing about your cycle to Health. The phase isn't stored (it is recalculated each time) and doesn't appear in widgets, on the Apple Watch or in notifications. Your period dates and flow don't leave the iPhone; the estimated phase is only sent with the "Cycle phase" permission (section 3). When you turn it off, AOVI stops reading those days and that permission is withdrawn.

  • PHQ-9 and GAD-7 are only used inside the app to soften the suggestions on a difficult day: they are never shown or sent.
  • We don't use Health data for advertising or marketing. We don't sell it. We don't store it in iCloud.
  • Only summaries leave the device, with the "Health signals" permission, and your estimated cycle phase, with the "Cycle phase" permission (section 3).
  • You can revoke access at any time in the iOS Health app.

5 · What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal data.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't share data with ad networks.
  • We don't include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We never send your journal, your notes or the titles of your calendar events.

6 · Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. We don't receive or store your payment details. The app only knows your subscription status. AOVI PRO is only unlocked by an App Store purchase. If you have an account, the purchase identifier is shared with our server.

7 · Diagnostics and support

Anonymous diagnostics (on by default)

To find and fix problems, the app sends our server:

  • crash, hang and performance reports from the system (Apple's MetricKit);
  • error codes, with the name of the screen where they happened;
  • with each upload: a random installation identifier, the app version, the system, the iPhone model, the language, and whether the app came from TestFlight or the App Store.

They don't include your name, email, account, texts or health data, and the installation identifier is not linked to your account. They are kept for 30 days. You can turn them off in Profile → Advanced → Anonymous diagnostics; when you do, the app discards anything still waiting to be sent.

Feature usage (only if you agree)

If you agree at the welcome screen or in Profile → Advanced, the app also sends daily usage counts with no content: how many times you open the app, do the check-in, complete an action or open a notification, and who answered the Coach (our server, Apple's AI or none). Never which task, which mood or what you wrote. They use the same installation identifier and can be turned off the same way.

Support bundle

If you ask us for help, you can generate a support bundle in Profile → Support and diagnostics. It is only created when you ask, and only leaves your iPhone if you send it. It contains technical information: version, settings, permissions, recent errors and diagnostics. Logs mask emails, credentials and tokens, and never include the body of network requests.

8 · Retention and deletion

  • Signing out disconnects the account from the server. Your data stays on the device; it is deleted if another account signs in or if you delete yours.
  • Deleting the account from Settings deletes your account and its data on the server and, after that, the data on this device. If the server doesn't confirm the deletion, the app tells you and deletes nothing.
  • What the AI assistant remembers is deleted separately, with Clear Coach memory in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally.
  • You can revoke access to Health, calendar, microphone or location in iOS Settings.

Where to find them: Profile → Account and AI → Your data, with "Delete account and data" and "Clear Coach memory". If you use AOVI without an account, "Delete account and data" deletes the data on this iPhone. Deleting the account doesn't cancel your App Store subscription: cancel it separately in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

The journal and Presence photos use a key that only exists on this iPhone: they don't move to another iPhone with a backup.

9 · Your rights

If the General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your personal data. Write to tomadrales@hotmail.com and we will reply within the legal deadline. You can also complain to the supervisory authority in your country. In Spain, that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

The legal basis is the performance of the service you ask for (the account, the copy and the subscription); for health data and sending data to the AI, your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time; for usage counts, your consent; and for anonymous diagnostics, our legitimate interest in keeping the app working, which you can object to by turning them off.

You can take your data with you from the app: Profile → Account and AI → Your data has the PDF report for your doctor and your full progress as a CSV file.

10 · Children

AOVI is not intended for anyone under sixteen, and we don't knowingly collect data from people under that age.

11 · Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date and version at the top. Changes that expand how your data is used will ask for your consent again inside the app.
